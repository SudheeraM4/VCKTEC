Technology is better

Vidyartha College Kandy Technology Society (VCKTEC) brings students together to explore technology, build practical skills, and collaborate on creative projects. Through club activities, workshops, and events, members learn by making and sharing ideas.

We are a place to ask questions, try something new, share what you discover, and turn ideas into projects alongside your fellow students.
